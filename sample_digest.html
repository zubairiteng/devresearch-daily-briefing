Renormalization for Robust Memory without Synchronization
arXiv • Score: 80/100
Local error correction can preserve classical information against noise, but protection under synchronized updates need not survive when sites evolve at independent random times. Establishing stability is particularly challenging when protection relies on the collective action of competing local rules. Here we develop a spacetime renormalization framework that reveals how independently acting local updates can collectively stabilize memory.

ShallowDefend: Harnessing Shallow Safety Alignment for Multi-Turn Defender
arXiv • Score: 95/100
Safety alignment has enabled large language models to reliably refuse harmful requests directly, yet the same request distributed across a multi-turn conversation still succeeds far too often. This brittleness has a mechanistic root: Alignment is shallow, with safety behavior concentrated in a model's first few output tokens, and existing defenses inherit the weakness, supervising the surface form of the response without ever giving the model an explicit safety decision to learn. We take the opposite view of shallowness: if a short span determines the trajectory of an entire response, that span is the highest-leverage place to intervene, and the defender can occupy it first.
Relevance: Useful for your NLP lectures and ongoing publications on efficient model architectures.
Possible research gap / opportunity: Possible publication opportunity: Investigating sample efficiency and domain adaptation across low-resource dialects or localized educational datasets.
Entanglement diagnostics of stochastic-resetting steady states: theory and measurement on superconducting qubits
arXiv • Score: 95/100
Stochastic resetting drives quantum systems into nonequilibrium stationary states unreachable under uninterrupted unitary evolution, and their entanglement depends on whether the reset history is resolved or averaged over. We study this interplay in the transverse-field Ising chain, deriving closed-form results for two qubits and evaluating chains of up to 12 qubits exactly. In the two-qubit system, these closed-form results show that phase matching between energy eigenstates produces resonances, and their visibility in the stationary state is determined by the symmetry of the reset state.

Read, Allocate, Route: Role-Coupled Tokenization for Pixel-Space Generative Transformers
arXiv • Score: 95/100
Pixel-space generative Transformers typically use one token per image patch, so capturing finer detail requires more tokens and makes global attention increasingly expensive. We observe that finer detail does not necessarily require more global communication: low-frequency structure benefits from global exchange, while high-frequency detail can be modeled locally. Based on this insight, we introduce SpecSlot, a pixel-space rectified-flow Transformer that decouples output detail from the length of the global attention sequence.

Rethinking Neural Thickets Without Weight Perturbations
arXiv • Score: 95/100
The Neural Thickets hypothesis posits that pretrained language models lie in dense neighborhoods of task-specific experts in weight space, which random Gaussian perturbations can uncover. We reproduce the reported gains, but show that they do not require this geometric explanation. Perturbing a single decoder block already recovers much of the improvement, while keeping the model entirely frozen and adding a random bias to its output logits matches or exceeds full-weight perturbations on most reasoning tasks.

Reconstructing hidden observables via quantum snapshot thermometry
arXiv • Score: 95/100
Projective snapshots are a standard readout tool for quantum simulators, but they directly probe only observables that are diagonal in the measurement basis. We show how local equilibration and an independently calibrated equilibrium equation of state can reveal otherwise hidden conserved-density profiles. We demonstrate numerically that such ``quantum snapshot thermometry'' can recover energy fronts and a diffusion constant, distinguish spin transport from energy spreading, and reveal conserved-charge profiles invisible in the initial measurements.
